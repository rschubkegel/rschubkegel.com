It's been around a while

- Mentioned in Rigveda\* (~2000 BC) <!-- .element class="fragment" -->
- Choice drink in ancient Greece (~300 BC) <!-- .element class="fragment" -->
- Drunk by warriors in Beowulf (~600 AD)  <!-- .element class="fragment" -->

NOTES:
- Rigveda is ancient Indian collection of hymns
- Rigveda mentions "soma" which is a ritual drink associated with immortality
- Mentioned by Aristotle
- Danish warriors; orally-transmitted poem
